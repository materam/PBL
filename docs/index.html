A home screen that asks why.

Mindful is a free Android launcher. Your apps become plain words, and before a distracting app opens, it asks you to wait a few seconds and write down why you're opening it.

What happens when you tap Instagram

  1. You wait ten seconds.A slow breathing circle counts down. Often that is enough to notice you were opening it out of habit.
  2. You write your reason.“Reply to Riya's message” is a reason. The button stays locked until you write one.
  3. You choose how long.Pick 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before the app opens.
  4. Mindful reminds you when time is up.A quiet notification tells you the session is over and offers a way back home.

You decide which apps get a pause. Everything else opens instantly, so calling, maps and banking never slow you down.

Everything else is quiet on purpose

  • Seven words on your home screenUp to seven apps, as text. A clock and the date. No icons, badges or colours pulling at you.
  • Search first, scroll lastSwipe up and start typing. Press enter to open the top result.
  • Focus hoursSet quiet hours, like 10 pm to 7 am. Paused apps then ask you to wait thirty seconds instead of ten.
  • Your reasons, written downEvery reason you type is kept in a log, so you can look back at why you actually reach for your phone.
  • Hide what you don't needRemove apps from the list without uninstalling them.

Privacy

Mindful can't send your data anywhere. It has no internet permission.

Your app list, your reasons and your daily counts are stored only on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Uninstalling Mindful deletes all of it.

Install it in two minutes

  1. Download the APK on your phone.mindful-launcher.apk from the latest release.
  2. Allow the install.Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Allow it once.
  3. Open Mindful.Swipe up to see your apps. Hold an app to add it to your home screen or turn on its pause.
  4. Make it your home screen.Hold an empty part of the home screen, open Settings, and choose “Set Mindful as your home app”.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Mindful is free and its code is open source under the MIT licence.

Why isn't it on the Play Store yet?

It's an early release. For now you install it from GitHub. A Play Store version is planned.

Can I go back to my old home screen?

Yes. Open your phone's Settings, search for “Default apps”, and pick your old launcher under Home app.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. iOS doesn't allow third-party home screens, so Mindful is Android only.

Can I skip the pause?

You can always tap “never mind, take me home”. To open the app, you wait and write a reason. That small effort is the point.
